BEHIND THE TECHNOLOGY

Meet the intelligence behind ReviewSense.

A machine learning application that turns movie reviews into meaningful sentiment predictions.

✦

Our Dataset

Trained using the IMDb movie review dataset, containing 50,000 labeled reviews.

⌘

Text Processing

Reviews are cleaned and transformed into numerical features using TF-IDF with unigrams and bigrams.

◎

Machine Learning

A tuned Linear Support Vector Machine (Linear SVM) classifies reviews as positive or negative.

ⓘ

Understanding the model's limitations

ReviewSense was trained on IMDb movie reviews. Its predictions may be less reliable for other types of text, sarcasm, mixed opinions, or language outside its training data. Its decision score is not a calibrated confidence percentage.
